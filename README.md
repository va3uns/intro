👋 Hi, I’m Varun Sangani

I’m a designer and researcher based in Düsseldorf, Germany. I started in architecture in India and moved into human-computer interaction (M.Sc. Usability Engineering, Hochschule Rhein-Waal). Today I work as a designer at Planwerkstatt, turning the brand experiences of automotive and other premium brands into trade-fair spaces.

## What I’m interested in

- **Politics and public space:** how politics, religion and culture shape who can use a space, and who can’t
- **Social interaction:** how the spaces and systems we design change the way people meet
- **Speculative design and design fiction:** imagining futures before we build them
- **Human-centred design:** keeping people at the centre of decisions
- **Emerging technology in public life:** how autonomous systems will shape our streets and encounters
- **Spatial and communication design:** exhibitions, trade fairs and temporary spaces

## Projects

- **[Wetterbank](https://va3uns.github.io/wetterbank/):** a design fiction about a smart bench that starts conversations
- **KaLink:** a community app for international students and long-time residents of Kamp-Lintfort
- **Spatialising Protests:** my Bachelor’s thesis on the idea of a “designated protest place” in New Delhi
